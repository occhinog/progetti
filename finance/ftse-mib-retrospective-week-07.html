Lessons and unresolved evidence

The week confirms that the published range and 15-minute rule produced an eligible paper entry and a positive paper result. It contradicts any categorical claim that a week without a verified in-window issuer results catalyst must lose; it does not validate Enel selection, confirmation, or a predictive edge. No trade would have avoided the exposure and missed this modelled €7.87 gain. The [exchange’s weekly Enel figure](https://www.borsaitaliana.it/borsa/indici/performance-ftsemib-week.html?lang=it) uses a different window and price convention from the Monday 10:15-to-Friday 12:00 paper trade; a matched-window FTSE MIB and utility-sector return, event expectations, broker confirmations and actual costs remain unavailable. The prior selection, stronger-confirmation and binding-adverse-exit hypotheses remain unresolved; no strategy change follows.
