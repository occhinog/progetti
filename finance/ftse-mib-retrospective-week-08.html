Lessons against prior hypotheses

This paper gain contradicts a categorical rule that a stock without a verified in-week issuer-results catalyst must lose. It also preserves the contrary no-trade case: concentrated exposure and unverified directional edge were real concerns, although holding cash would have missed this modeled gain before costs. The 15-minute gate admitted this trade; without a predeclared, same-week counterfactual it cannot be credited for the gain. The adverse-exit hypothesis was not tested by a binding exit. Selection, timing, execution and market effects cannot be causally separated from one week.
