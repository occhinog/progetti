Selection

The AMP thesis was published on 12 October at 00:08:55 CEST, before the 09:00 deadline. It sought a confirmed pullback after the 8 October GN Hearing valuation disclosure. The proposed 56 million-share issuance and financing risk were known; VibeTrading preferred no trade, and Gabriel selected AMP conditionally. Execution and cash

The ledger models 69 shares bought at €12.645 on Monday 17:15:16 CEST and sold at €12.31 on Friday 12:00:01 CEST, strictly after noon. With zero assumed fees and taxes, entry cost rounded half-up is €872.51, residual cash €3.23, proceeds €849.39, and simulated gross and net loss €23.12. Market effect and exit timing

After the modeled entry, the observed holding-window high was €12.79 on Tuesday, below the thesis's €12.86 recovery reference. Thursday's 09:00 minute reached €12.10, below the €12.185 analytical invalidation; strategy v1.2 still required Friday's fixed exit. Friday's later exchange value was €12.555, above the €12.31 noon proxy: the subsequent recovery is contrary evidence to treating the noon loss as the whole-day outcome. It is not an executable alternative fill. Borsa Italiana reports AMP down 0.87% on its official weekly-price basis versus FTSE MIB up 1.13% close to close. Those windows and price conventions differ from the Monday 17:15-to-Friday-noon paper trade; a matched-window index return was unavailable. Lessons and unresolved attribution

Week five confirms that the published 15-minute gate can be satisfied by a late pullback followed by a loss. The earlier no-trade preference would have avoided this particular simulated loss, but one hindsight comparison does not establish a selection edge. The analytical invalidation was crossed after entry, yet no binding early exit existed; a sale at the threshold cannot be assumed. The five simulated weeks now total −€147.38, leaving €852.62 from €1,000 and a 14.74% closing-cash drawdown. This supports continued prospective tracking of selection, confirmation and exit hypotheses without approving a strategy change. September US CPI was released Wednesday at 14:30 CEST, reporting +0.6% month on month; no causal contribution to AMP's move was established.
