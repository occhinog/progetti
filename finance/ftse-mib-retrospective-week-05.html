Lessons and unresolved attribution

Week five confirms that the published 15-minute gate can be satisfied by a late pullback followed by a loss. The earlier no-trade preference would have avoided this particular simulated loss, but one hindsight comparison does not establish a selection edge. The analytical invalidation was crossed after entry, yet no binding early exit existed; a sale at the threshold cannot be assumed. The five simulated weeks now total −€147.38, leaving €852.62 from €1,000 and a 14.74% closing-cash drawdown. This supports continued prospective tracking of selection, confirmation and exit hypotheses without approving a strategy change.
