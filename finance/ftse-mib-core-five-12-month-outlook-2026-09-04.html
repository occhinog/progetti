Evidence vs. inference

  • Evidence: 11 September prices, share counts, reported half-year results, management guidance, issuer-published consensus and calendar dates are linked to official issuer or Borsa Italiana sources.
  • Inference: market capitalisation, valuation lenses, scenario assumptions, probability weights and 12-month price ranges are analyst-built calculations. They are deliberately not presented as facts or price targets.
  • List change: Prysmian remains operationally strong but closed at €127.25, above the prior probability-weighted €122.50 estimate. It is removed from preferred purchases on valuation, not business quality. ENI replaces it with lower price-only upside but stronger visible distributions.
  • Central trade-off: Generali, Intesa and Poste offer earnings and capital-return visibility; Italgas adds regulated growth with leverage risk; ENI adds cash returns and commodity upside with materially higher macro volatility.
  • Next action: revisit this report after the October–November results cycle. Promote only names whose operating results, valuation and catalyst still support the base case; otherwise invalidate the thesis.
