Learning for the next selection

Keep

  • The published entry ceiling prevented a chase above €51.00.
  • The single-position rule prevented averaging into a failing setup.
  • The fixed Friday exit kept the result finite and reproducible.

Change

  • A short-week rebound thesis without a dated catalyst needs stronger confirmation or a lower probability.
  • State explicitly whether “invalidation” is analytical only or an executable stop; the current rules allow only the Friday exit.
  • Continue to preserve residual cash and distinguish broker-confirmed facts from market-data estimates.
