Why Leonardo

Evidence

  • Leonardo closed at €50.45 on 18 September, after a €50.12–€51.13 session range; one-month and six-month performance were −15.51% and −20.33% respectively.
  • Its 1Q 2026 disclosure reported €9bn of new orders (+31% year-on-year), €4.4bn revenue (+7%), €281m EBITA (+33%) and a €57bn backlog (+23%).
  • The 2026–30 industrial plan is a defined multi-year operating framework, but its benefits are not a catalyst scheduled for this week.

Inference and trade-off

  • The Friday close near the day’s low provides a clearer, measurable rebound setup than buying a recent high.
  • There is no scheduled reporting event before Friday; this is primarily a technical/positioning thesis, so the probability is lower than for a dated earnings catalyst.
  • Defence-sector news can reprice the share rapidly in either direction. The entry cap and invalidation are more important than the upside objective.
