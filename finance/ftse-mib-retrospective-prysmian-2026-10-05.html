Process lessons

Keep

  • Check publication time against the actual Monday 09:00 CEST cutoff; the report was live at 00:59:34 CEST.
  • Apply the stated whole-share sizing and Friday exit consistently, even after the thesis fails.
  • Preserve broker-versus-simulation provenance.

Improve

  • The strong prior Friday did not carry through the following Monday. Require better evidence of sustained relative strength before assigning the full weekly budget to a momentum thesis.
  • Make the distinction between analytical invalidation and the binding Friday time exit explicit when presenting downside exposure.
  • Stress-test downside zones against recent single-stock ranges; the observed low passed the stated €123.15 level.
