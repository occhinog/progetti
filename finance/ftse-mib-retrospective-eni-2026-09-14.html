Learning for the next selection

Keep

  • Conditional entry avoided buying above the published ceiling.
  • The fixed Friday exit produced an auditable, finite result.
  • Keeping residual cash explicit made the balance reproducible.

Change

  • Buyback support is not a price floor; it should remain supporting evidence, not the central short-week catalyst.
  • A weekly objective needs a dated catalyst or stronger confirmation than a macro-sensitive oil thesis alone.
  • Continue to use an entry cap and time exit; do not average down after an invalidation.
