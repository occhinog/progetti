Market and peer context

Borsa Italiana's comparable weekly table shows PST down 5.39% from 25 September to 2 October, while the FTSE MIB fell about 2.7%. Selected FTSE MIB financial-services peers also declined: Azimut 3.29%, FinecoBank 1.01% and Banca Mediolanum 1.03%. Poste's decline was therefore larger than these peers and the broad index; the comparison does not isolate the TIM offer as its cause. The share's holding-window range was €26.22 to €24.20, about 7.7% of the high, indicating a wide one-week range. A comparable sector-index return and implied-volatility measure were not checked.
