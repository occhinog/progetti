Lessons and unresolved evidence

This week confirms that a dated issuer event and valid 15-minute entry can coincide with a winning paper trade. It contradicts any categorical claim that the known Friday gap risk made this ENI week a loser; it leaves the selection, stronger-confirmation and binding-adverse-exit hypotheses in the [prior learning record](file:///home/perymgael/finance/docs/strategy-retrospective-memory-2026-10-12.md) unproven. The no-trade alternative would have missed this modelled gain while avoiding the adverse-event exposure. Six simulated weeks leave €891.82 from €1,000, down 10.82%, with a 14.74% maximum weekly closing-cash drawdown; no broker performance is verified. The supplied 12–16 October €43–45 market_path is incompatible with ENI’s exchange prices and is excluded from this attribution. Same-window sector and commodity data, pre-release expectations, transaction costs and broker confirmations are missing. Week six remains governed by its published version 1.2 rules; version 1.3 applies prospectively.
