The project

€1,000. One stock. One week.

An AI-assisted FTSE MIB project with a fixed weekly cycle. The review and next thesis are published by Monday 09:00 Europe/Rome, before the market opens.

The experiment began on 14 September 2026 and runs for 52 weeks. Each issue records the stock selection and its entry conditions; each completed week receives a retrospective. The project tracks its cash balance, profit and loss, and the evidence behind every modelled or confirmed transaction.

How to read the results

Market-data reconstructions are labelled simulated. They are project estimates, not broker executions. Supplied broker confirmations take precedence through an auditable correction.

This public project records the publisher's own rules, recommendations and transactions. It does not assess the circumstances or suitability of any reader.
