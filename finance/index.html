Fixed rules

1 · Select before Monday 09:00AI-assisted analysis selects one FTSE MIB share using public information. The publisher validates the evidence and publishes the thesis before the Monday opening.
2 · Buy on MondayInvest the available project cash during Monday's regular session, only under entry conditions published before 09:00. If those conditions cannot be met on Monday, record no trade.
3 · Hold one positionNo additional shares, averaging down, leverage, short selling or derivatives. The position remains unchanged during the week.
4 · Sell on FridayClose the full position at the first available execution after 12:00 Europe/Rome, regardless of profit or loss. A market closure or suspension is documented as an exception.
5 · Report the full resultThe next Sunday digest records the platform, instrument, quantity, execution timestamps and prices, fees, taxes, gross result, net result and remaining project cash.
6 · Preserve the recordEvery issue remains accessible. Changes are timestamped; losing weeks and non-executed recommendations are never removed.

52-week clarification · 5–6 October 2026

Duration and capital. Week 1 is 14–18 September 2026; week 52 ends 10 September 2027, with final review on 12 September. No-trade weeks count. Preserve current capital and history; the €1,000 starting balance is never reset. No new position is proposed after week 52.

Whole-share compounding. At a qualifying Monday entry, buy the maximum affordable integer quantity: floor(available cash / entry price) with zero fees. With fees, choose the largest quantity whose full cost fits. Retain residual cash. After the full Friday exit, carry sale proceeds plus residual cash into the next week. Prices retain source precision; monetary postings round half-up to cents.

Evidence and timing. Supplied prices represent user-confirmed executions; missing quantity, timestamp, fees or taxes remain pending reconciliation. Broker evidence overrides simulation through an auditable correction. Otherwise use validated, cached timestamped exchange data, evaluate published 15-minute entry confirmation and cancellations chronologically, and prefer the first eligible trade or the first subsequent completed one-minute close inside the band. Exit strictly after Friday noon Europe/Rome. Unverified timestamps, missing observations or ambiguous sequencing remain unresolved. Historical exceptions are preserved.

6 October paper-trade default. The pre-open proposal states one indicative price and whole-share quantity inside its allowed range. If Gabriel supplies no broker fill, a qualifying Monday entry is modelled at the first eligible observed exchange price, with quantity recalculated from available cash; it may differ from the pre-open indication. The Friday exit is likewise modelled from the first eligible exchange observation after noon. These are simulated project transactions, not confirmed broker activity. A complete Monday session without a qualifying entry is recorded as no trade and reviewed in the following retrospective. Missing or ambiguous market evidence remains unresolved; no fill is invented. Later broker evidence replaces a simulation through an auditable correction.

Weekly process. From ~/finance, use finance run. The terminal offers to record actual broker buy and sell details; pressing Enter keeps the labelled simulation. One manual run processes due entry, exit, ledger, retrospective, next selection, challenge and publication stages. Python handles bookkeeping and rendering. Codex reviews the result, VibeTrading compares candidates, and Codex challenges the proposal. Routine ledger updates may publish automatically; exact pre-open analysis is shown for Gabriel’s approval before publication. Scheduling is deferred.

Learning and finish line. Preserve contradictory evidence and version strategies; changes need prospective approval. A profitable week alone does not establish improvement. Final review reconciles cash, return, weekly closing-cash drawdown and evidence quality. Actual subscription costs are recorded separately without reducing trading capital. Compare final cash minus €1,000 with supplied costs; missing costs mean coverage is not assessed.
