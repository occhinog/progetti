Public investment experiment · Rules v1.0

€1,000. One stock. One week.

An AI-assisted FTSE MIB project with a fixed weekly cycle. Every Sunday, the digest publishes the verified result of the previous trade and the selected stock and execution conditions for the coming week.

FTSE MIB only Sunday publication Monday purchase Friday exit

Fixed rules

1 · Select on SundayAI-assisted analysis selects one FTSE MIB share using public information. The publisher validates the evidence and remains responsible for the recommendation.
2 · Buy on MondayInvest the available project cash during Monday's regular session, only under the entry conditions published on Sunday. If those conditions cannot be met on Monday, record no trade.
3 · Hold one positionNo additional shares, averaging down, leverage, short selling or derivatives. The position remains unchanged during the week.
4 · Sell on FridayClose the full position at the first available execution after 12:00 Europe/Rome, regardless of profit or loss. A market closure or suspension is documented as an exception.
5 · Report the full resultThe next Sunday digest records the platform, instrument, quantity, execution timestamps and prices, fees, taxes, gross result, net result and remaining project cash.
6 · Preserve the recordEvery issue remains accessible. Changes are timestamped; losing weeks and non-executed recommendations are never removed.

Publication and MAR record

The only scheduled publication is the Sunday digest. Each recommendation states the responsible publisher, completion and publication times, price source and timestamp, current position, intended transaction, methodology, assumptions, risks, conflicts, validity period and planned update frequency.

Material factual errors may be corrected outside the Sunday schedule. The original issue remains archived with a dated correction notice.

Framework: Market Abuse Regulation and Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/958.

This public project records the publisher's own rules, recommendations and transactions. It does not assess the circumstances or suitability of any reader.
