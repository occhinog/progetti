ENI: the cleanest 14–18 September setup

A five-session tactical research note. Research date: 14 September 2026; price cut-off: official close on 11 September 2026.

Research, not a transaction instruction. The levels and probabilities below are analyst-built inferences. They exclude tax, dealing costs, execution quality, portfolio fit and individual risk tolerance.
Primary research pick: ENI

The setup is valid only near €23.85–€24.10. Above €24.30, the prospective reward no longer compensates adequately for the downside condition.

Why ENI

Evidence

  • ENI closed at €24.00 on 11 September after trading between €23.75 and €24.205.
  • The 2026 buyback was expanded to €3.4bn. ENI purchased €75m of shares in the latest disclosed week, at a €23.5523 weighted-average price.
  • Management estimates approximately €0.11bn of cash-flow sensitivity for each $1 change in Brent.
  • The first 2026 dividend tranche is scheduled to trade ex-dividend on Monday 21 September.

Inference

  • Elevated crude prices give ENI a clearer short-term earnings tailwind than most FTSE MIB constituents.
  • The buyback provides recurring demand but is not a guaranteed price floor.
  • Demand ahead of the ex-dividend date may support Friday's session, although the dividend is already public information.
  • Leonardo has stronger recent momentum but a less attractive fresh entry after its latest advance.

One-week scenarios

55%Positive: ENI tests €24.30–€24.70 while crude and risk sentiment remain supportive.
25%Range-bound: the share ends the week close to the €24 starting point.
20%Negative: oil reverses, geopolitical risk eases or ENI breaks below €23.65.

What would change the conclusion

Oil reversal. A decisive Brent move below $100 would weaken the main near-term operating catalyst.

Unfavourable opening. A gap above €24.30 would consume much of the forecast upside before the position window begins.

Price failure. A break below €23.65 would invalidate the technical support assumption; the weekly thesis should not be extended into a longer holding period merely to avoid recognising failure.
