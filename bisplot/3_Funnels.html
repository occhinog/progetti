How to read a rate here

The denominator changes stage to stage — a repeat attendee adds attendance but adds no contact — so every rate names its own base in the table.

Registration rate = registrations ÷ landing visits · Attendance = live ÷ registrations for that session · Repeat = returning ÷ live attendees · Net new contacts = first-time ÷ registrations · Signpost CTR = clicks ÷ contacts emailed · Event fill = tickets ÷ seats · Win rate = won ÷ proposals sent.

Why the asymmetry works — and the one fix it needs

The recording is public, so registering buys nothing except the right to ask. That is honest and free to maintain.

Pin a comment and description line on every upload: “questa è una registrazione; per fare domande, iscriviti alla prossima sessione live su sai.wiki”. Without it an unanswered comment section reads as neglect rather than policy, and the back-catalogue captures nothing.

Attendance is set at 35%, not 40%, because the full session goes out openly — reach is bought with attendance, deliberately.

Account signal — what the azienda field buys

Trigger propose: 2+ contacts from one firm, or 1 across 3+ webinars. The signpost only catches self-declared intent; this catches interest nobody voiced. On an SME sale the webinar attendee is usually not the signer.

Economics — the loss, stated plainly

Revenue structurally none; events and sponsorship are occasional offsets, not a stream. Cost: domain € 90, two productions, uncovered F&B, marketing € ?.

cost per contact = total SAI cost ÷ net new contacts — judged against what a contact is worth once BusinessAI converts it.

The ticket page is a priced surface: no mascot, no .wiki pricing. Luma hosting already resolves this.

Spin-out cap table — read it through, not at face value

At incorporation: Bisplot ≥ 60%, creator-CEO ≥ 20%, remainder to investors.

The creator is also a Bisplot partner, so at floor values they hold 20% directly plus a third of the holding’s 60% — an effective 40%, against 20% for each partner who only advised. That doubling is the premium incentive, and it is what puts the structure inside normal venture-studio range.

Every product is addressed to the creator and to HouseOfVibes by Bisplot. Partial acquisitions always through an SPV, so one failure cannot reach the holding.
