04

No mascot

BusinessAI
Per professionisti e PMI
The character that isn't there
BusinessAI carries no mascot. Milo and Mila belong to the free tier, Semino to the ventures; on a paid proposal either one undercuts the price. Where the other brands put a character, BusinessAI puts the badge, the rule and the descriptor.
The exception is a co-branded SAI → BusinessAI funnel page, where Milo may appear on the SAI half only.
05

Rules

Do
Navy as the only accent. One per surface, on the action or the label, not both.
7px radius on every card, button and image well; the navy square under the mark stays square.
State the commitment in words: ore, settimane, posti. A course page without hours is a brochure.
Don't
No gradients, no decorative shadows, no navy type on navy ground.
No SmartAI blue and no Vibes green — the navy is what separates paid from free.
No mascots, no free-tier language. "Gratis" never appears on a BusinessAI surface.
Relationship
SAI opens the door and hands over; BusinessAI teaches and then consults; HouseOfVibes builds what the consultancy learns is missing.
