Bisplot S.r.l. · Business model

How the group earns

Bisplot owns the companies and signs the invoices. Three brands face the market: SAI opens the door, BusinessAI sells the work, HouseOfVibes builds the products. This sheet sets out how each one makes money, what it costs to run, and on what terms value returns to the holding.

The group funnel

One acquisition path, three monetisation points. A professional meets the group on a free surface and is carried across — a .wiki page never quotes a paid price, it links to the academy. Each brand then runs its own funnel inside this one; they are set out brand by brand below.

01 · Attract sai.wiki

Free for all. Webinar, podcast, reel — zero cost to the audience. Aperitivi and dinners are ticketed at cost recovery. Builds the credibility the academy sells against.

02 · Convert businessai.academy

The cash engine. Modular courses from € 2.500, fundable through formazione finanziata, opening into consultancy engagements of € 10–45k.

03 · Compound houseofvibes.ai

The asset builder. Holds the group’s software stack and runs the startups. Converts service cash and in-house capability into equity.

Open question — sponsorship

A hosted edition is the one sponsorship format that does not damage the brand: a venue, association or vendor wants the event live in presence at their site and covers venue, F&B and a fee, while SAI keeps full control of the content and the stage. That converts the events line from cost-recovery to contribution without ever putting a price on a .wiki surface. Cash-for-audience media sponsorship is the format to refuse — it turns the free surface into a sales surface and destroys exactly the credibility that makes the funnel work. Formazione finanziata

The training leg qualifies as funded training, routed through a partner — aboutJob. This is the price lever that matters: it lowers the client’s net outlay without lowering our invoice, which makes the € 2.500 entry point far easier to clear and pulls the consultancy proposal forward. Status: partner not yet engaged — aboutJob still to be contacted, and no second partner exists. Until that agreement is signed, every course is sold at full cost to the client and the model above is optimistic.

Build the panel to four. One partner is a single point of failure on the academy’s best pricing lever, and in Italy accreditation is regional — each Region keeps its own list of accredited bodies, so a single partner also means a single territory. Target a second, third and fourth partner chosen for regional coverage rather than for terms, working from the regional lists; the marginal cost of an extra partner agreement is near zero and each one opens a territory the others cannot serve. Delivery principle

External SaaS preferred to internal tools. We recommend and configure third-party software rather than building our own for client work — no build cost, no maintenance liability, no support obligation carried on our P&L after the engagement closes. Internal tooling stays inside HouseOfVibes, where it is an asset rather than a deliverable.

Attribution

Every product is addressed to the creator and to HouseOfVibes by Bisplot. The creator is never anonymised by the house brand, and the house brand never appears without the holding behind it.

Acquisitions

Any acquisition of a startup is done through an SPV, unless it is a 100% acquisition — in which case it can be taken directly. The SPV ring-fences minority deals so a single failure cannot reach the holding or the other brands.

Spin-out cap table — at incorporation

When a product goes to market as a standalone company from the start:

Bisplot≥ 60%
Creator — CEO≥ 20%
Investors / partners≤ 20% — the remainder

The creator sits in the holding as well. The direct stake is only half the incentive — the creator is one of the three Bisplot partners, so they also own a third of the holding’s 60%. Read through, at the floor values:

Creator — direct20%
Creator — via Bisplot (33,3% of 60%)20%
Creator — effective40%
Each other partner — effective20%
Investors / partners20%

That 40% is the premium incentive: whoever builds the thing ends up with double the look-through of the partners who advised on it, while the group still consolidates the company. It is also what makes the structure fundable — a CEO at an effective 40% is inside normal venture-studio range, which a nominal 20% alone would not be.
