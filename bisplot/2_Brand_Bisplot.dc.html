Ownership

Bisplot S.r.l. is the sole owner of the three brands, their domains and the products built under them. The brands have no separate legal personality; they are trading names with their own identity and their own audience.

This keeps one set of books, one VAT number and one counterparty for every client, while leaving each brand free to speak in the register its market expects.

At a glance
One entity
Bisplot S.r.l., Milano
Three brands
SAI, BusinessAI, HouseOfVibes
Three partners
Davide, Gabriel, Matteo

Invoicing

Quotes may carry a brand's name and typography. The invoice never does: it is issued by Bisplot S.r.l. with the holding's letterhead, and references the brand only in the line description.

The brand proposes the work. The holding is accountable for it.
