Bisplot S.r.l. · Mascotte

Three characters, two brands

Milo and Mila teach; Semino builds. Each sheet is the same rig — 8 columns, 11 rows, 192 × 208 px per frame — so the three share one animation vocabulary even though they belong to different brands. BusinessAI and Bisplot have no mascot, and will not get one.

The cast
Chi appartiene a chi
Milo SAI.wiki
The white one, with the SAI headset. Hosts the free webinars: he is the voice that explains, so he gets the talking and pointing frames.
Mila SAI.wiki
The lavender one, with the SAI badge. Takes the learner's side: she asks the question the room is thinking, and reacts to the answer.
Semino houseofvibes.ai
A seed that grows with the product. Carries boxes rather than talking: he marks work in progress, shipping and launches.
Milo and Mila appear together whenever there is a dialogue to stage — a lesson, a Q&A, a comparison. Alone, Milo speaks and Mila listens. Semino never shares a frame with them: different brand, different surface.

Regole comuni
One character per surface, and never next to a price.
They do not speak in bubbles, hold logos, or wear anything beyond what the render already gives them. None of them appears on invoices, contracts, or anything carrying the Bisplot signature — the holding has no mascot. Keep them at 96 px or larger; below that they read as a smudge. Never mix a SAI character and Semino in the same frame.
