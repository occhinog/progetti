Vibes is always green
HouseOf in ink, Vibes in #0E7C66. On dark grounds Vibes steps up to #4FBFA2 and HouseOf goes white. One word is never set in the other's colour.
The rule and the square
A 1px green rule ending in a 5px square sits under the mark in the stacked lockup. It is the only ornament the brand owns; it never appears without the wordmark above it.
Clear space
The height of the cap H on all four sides. Minimum width 120px — the two words must stay legible as one line; the mark is never split across two lines.

04

Semino

Seme, germoglio, raccolto
Semino belongs to HouseOfVibes alone and changes state with the product it stands next to: seed for an idea, sprout for first revenue, harvest for a venture that has left the house. One state per surface, never next to a term sheet, never holding the wordmark.
Rig: 144 × 156 per frame, 8 columns, 11 rows. Full sheet and state list live in Mascotte.
05

Rules

Do
Green as the only accent. One per surface, on the action or the status, not both.
7px radius on every card, button and image well; the green square under the mark stays square.
Numbers in the open: day count, revenue, team size. A build log with no figures is a press release.
Don't
No gradients, no decorative shadows, no green type on green ground.
No Libre Baskerville or SmartAI blue — those belong to Bisplot and SAI, not here.
No Milo or Mila on a HouseOfVibes surface, and no Semino on a SAI one.
Relationship
HouseOfVibes builds and holds the ventures; BusinessAI sells the work, SAI opens the door.
Legal signature on any document: Bisplot S.r.l.
