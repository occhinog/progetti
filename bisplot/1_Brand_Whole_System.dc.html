Bisplot S.r.l. · Brand system

One company, four brands

Bisplot owns the companies and signs the invoices. Three brands face the market: SAI opens the door, BusinessAI sells the work, HouseOfVibes builds the products. Each has its own identity; all four share one grammar.

The shared grammar - visual here may differ from brand proposal in dedicated page of the project
The rule
A hairline closed by a solid square, in the brand's own accent, under every wordmark.
Paper
A warm off-white ground, shifted per brand. Never pure white, never a gradient.
The descriptor
Inter, 0.32em tracking, uppercase. Says plainly what the brand does, in Italian.
The signature
Bisplot always in Libre Baskerville, small, at the foot of the page. The only place the holding appears.
