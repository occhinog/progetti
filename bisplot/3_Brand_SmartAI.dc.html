05

Rules

Do
Blue as the only accent. One per surface, on the action or the label, not both.
7px radius on every card, button and image well.
Free-tier surfaces say so in words: Gratis, Aperto, Registrazione inclusa.
Don't
No gradients, no drop shadows for decoration, no blue-on-blue type.
No Libre Baskerville or warm paper — that palette belongs to Bisplot, not to SAI.wiki.
No paid-course pricing on a .wiki surface; link across to BusinessAI instead.
Relationship
SAI.wiki is the open face of SAI by TEN.M, trading as SmartAI. The design system is shared; only the tier differs.
Legal signature on any document: Bisplot S.r.l.
