Bisplot S.r.l. · Business model

How the group earns

Bisplot owns the companies and signs the invoices. Three brands face the market: SAI opens the door, BusinessAI sells the work, HouseOfVibes builds the products. This sheet sets out how each one makes money, what it costs to run, and on what terms value returns to the holding.

The group funnel

One acquisition path, three monetisation points. A professional meets the group on a free surface and is carried across — a .wiki page never quotes a paid price, it links to the academy. Each brand then runs its own funnel inside this one; they are set out brand by brand below.

01 · Attract sai.wiki

Free for all. Webinar, podcast, reel — zero cost to the audience. Aperitivi and dinners are ticketed at cost recovery. Builds the credibility the academy sells against.

02 · Convert businessai.academy

The cash engine. Two products: a modular course at € 500 a head, and a € 20.000 transformation package with the training included and funded.

03 · Compound houseofvibes.ai

The asset builder. Holds the group’s software stack and runs the startups. Converts service cash and in-house capability into equity.

How each brand makes money

Three different engines — cost recovery, fee-for-service, and equity. Only the middle one is expected to generate margin today.

Revenue from a free podcast and a free webinar

  • Guests who can buy. Twelve podcast slots a year — book clients, not personalities. Break-even is under one conversion.
  • Ordini e associazioni. A guest who controls a vertical opens its member list: one body of 2.000 members at 15% uptake is 300 contacts.
  • Hosted editions. A venue or partner pays to run the webinar or a live podcast in presence — they cover venue, F&B and a fee, we keep the content and the stage.
  • Sponsorship. Non-competing categories only — a bank, a co-working, an insurer. Never a vendor whose tool we teach.
  • Referrals. Affiliate on tools already taught, disclosed as link affiliato. Never inside a paid consultancy proposal — a € 20k problem is not worth € 1.200 of commission.
  • Ad spend displaced. Promoting the webinar on the podcast replaces Instagram budget — but cut it only against measured attribution, or you just lose registrations.
Where the 212 hours sit

Podcast 108 h (51%) · webinar 48 h (23%) · events 32 h (15%) · oversight 24 h (11%). One production a fortnight: week 1 webinar, week 3 podcast.

The podcast is half of SAI and captures no emails; the webinar does. Defensible as reach and as guest access — but that is the trade being made, and it should be made knowingly.

Cost per contact, and what SAI has to return

At 75% first-time registrations: 50 reg/webinar → € 22,05 · 100 → € 11,03 · 200 → € 5,51 per contact. Cheap at every volume for an opted-in B2B contact tagged by vertical and company.

Break-even is 1,2 course customers a year — a course customer being worth € 8.150 (a € 5.000 course plus a 45% proposal rate × 35% win × € 20.000 package). Two clear the brand twice over. Ad spend sets the rest: € 200/month puts SAI at € 9.924, € 100 at € 8.724, zero at € 7.524.

The constraint is calendars, not cash

€ 9.924 is trivial. 212 partner-hours is not. At a 20% commitment to Bisplot — 1.056 hours between the three of you — SAI eats 20,1% for a brand that earns nothing. At 35% it is 11,5%; at 50%, 8,0%.

For scale: one € 20k consultancy engagement is 200–270 hours at € 600–1.000/day — a whole year of SAI. Delivery capacity, not SAI, is the line that binds next.

Open question — sponsorship

A hosted edition is the one sponsorship format that does not damage the brand: a venue, association or vendor wants the event live in presence at their site and covers venue, F&B and a fee, while SAI keeps full control of the content and the stage. That converts the events line from cost-recovery to contribution without ever putting a price on a .wiki surface. Cash-for-audience media sponsorship is the format to refuse — it turns the free surface into a sales surface and destroys exactly the credibility that makes the funnel work. Dipendente or amministratore — the structure is worth € 35.000 a year

Three employee contracts cost € 246.253. The same € 180.000 paid as compenso amministratore under gestione separata costs the company roughly € 211.320 — a difference of € 34.933 a year, more than one client.

It is not free money: the employee route carries TFR, unemployment cover and a cleaner position for funded training, which matters when the whole price list depends on a fondo. Worth deciding deliberately with the commercialista rather than by default, because it moves the revenue target by a client and a bit.

The rotation only works if a client closes inside its month

At 60% margin a client is 265 hours, 133 each for the two partners on it — 3,8 weeks at 35 h/week. It fits, barely. At 50% margin it is 4,7 weeks and it does not fit: engagements overlap, the free month disappears, and all three are on delivery with nobody selling.

Fondo pre-approval sits between sold and delivered, so the sales calendar has to run ahead of the delivery calendar. The month a partner is free is the month they sell — that is the only slack in the design.

12 clients needs about double the pipeline of 6: 40 discovery calls a year at a 30% close, 3,3 a month. From SAI’s ~900 contacts that is 1,33% conversion against 0,67% at six clients, so the vertical campaigns have to carry more of it.

A larger package lets the fondo carry more of the bill

The bigger the package, the more of it can legitimately be training. On a € 30.000 package: € 5.000 of training takes the client to € 26.500; € 8.000 takes them to € 24.400; € 10.000 takes them to € 23.000 — 23% off, at no cost to us.

That is the lever that makes a five-figure price sayable to a small company. It only works where the training component is real and the client is eligible.

Half the vertical list may not qualify

Fondi interprofessionali finance employees. A company needs at least one dipendente and must be enrolled in a fondo; a libero professionista senza dipendenti cannot access them at all.

A PMI with ten staff qualifies, and so does a studio with salaried people — but an avvocato, commercialista or veterinario working alone or with collaboratori a partita IVA does not, and buys at full list. Ha dipendenti? is a discovery question, not an afterthought. The no-employee segment needs its own instrument which one?

The whole price list rests on an unsigned partner

The funding is routed through a partner — aboutJob, still not engaged, and no second partner exists. Until it is signed there is no funded price anywhere in the offer, whatever Davide and Matteo set the products at.

Build the panel to four. Accreditation is regional, so partner count is really territory count. Choose the second, third and fourth for coverage rather than terms. Priority regions list?

Attribution

Every product is addressed to the creator and to HouseOfVibes by Bisplot. The creator is never anonymised by the house brand, and the house brand never appears without the holding behind it.

Acquisitions

Any acquisition of a startup is done through an SPV, unless it is a 100% acquisition — in which case it can be taken directly. The SPV ring-fences minority deals so a single failure cannot reach the holding or the other brands.

Spin-out cap table — at incorporation

When a product goes to market as a standalone company from the start:

Bisplot≥ 60%
Creator — CEO≥ 20%
Investors / partners≤ 20% — the remainder

The creator sits in the holding as well. The direct stake is only half the incentive — the creator is one of the three Bisplot partners, so they also own a third of the holding’s 60%. Read through, at the floor values:

Creator — direct20%
Creator — via Bisplot (33,3% of 60%)20%
Creator — effective40%
Each other partner — effective20%
Investors / partners20%

That 40% is the premium incentive: whoever builds the thing ends up with double the look-through of the partners who advised on it, while the group still consolidates the company. It is also what makes the structure fundable — a CEO at an effective 40% is inside normal venture-studio range, which a nominal 20% alone would not be.
