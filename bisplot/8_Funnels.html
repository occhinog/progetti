How people move

Sheet 07 sets out how the group earns. This one sets out how someone gets from never having heard of us to signing something — brand by brand, with every rate tied to a stated base.

How to read a rate on this sheet

A percentage is meaningless without its denominator, and the denominator changes from stage to stage — a repeat attendee adds attendance but adds no contact. Every rate below names its own base. Targets are proposals to validate against the first measured cycle, not forecasts and not measurements.

Not a sales funnel — a contact engine. SAI sells nothing and is a permanent loss centre. Its product is the email pool; its only ask is a standing signpost to the academy. Judge it on cost per contact, never on revenue.

The asymmetry is the product. The recording is public, so registering buys you nothing except the ability to ask. That is honest and costs nothing to maintain. Make it explicit: pin a comment and a description line on every upload — “questa è una registrazione; per fare domande, iscriviti alla prossima sessione live su sai.wiki”. Without it an unanswered comment section reads as neglect rather than policy, and the back-catalogue captures nothing.

On an SME sale the person who attends a webinar is often not the person who signs. Repeat attendance from one firm is a buying signal that no individual ever has to declare — and it is the only thing the third registration field makes possible.

Economics — state the loss plainly

Revenue: structurally none. Events and sponsorships are occasional cost offsets and are deliberately not modelled as a stream. Cost: domain € 90, two productions, uncovered F&B, marketing — all € ?.

The one number: cost per contact acquired = total SAI cost ÷ net new contacts. Judged against what a contact is worth once BusinessAI converts it — never against revenue SAI itself produces.

Bisplot ≥ 60% · creator-CEO ≥ 20% · investors and partners take the remainder.

Read it through, not at face value. The creator is also one of the three Bisplot partners, so at the floor values they hold 20% directly plus a third of the holding’s 60% — an effective 40%, against 20% for each partner who only advised. That doubling is the premium incentive, and it is what puts the structure inside normal venture-studio range while the group still consolidates.
