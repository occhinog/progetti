# T=0 Player Category Board — 2026/27

Static auction board for Gabriel's 500-FM, eight-manager league.

## Scope

+-------------+------+-------+----------------------------------------------------------------+
| Role        | Core | Extra | Selection logic                                                |
+-------------+------+-------+----------------------------------------------------------------+
| Defenders   |   64 |     3 | Four starters + four reserves; extra sub-6 durability watch    |
| Midfielders |   64 |     1 | Four starters + four reserves; extra sub-6 durability watch    |
| Attackers   |   48 |     1 | Three starters + three reserves; extra sub-6 durability watch  |
+-------------+------+-------+----------------------------------------------------------------+

Formation priority: **4-3-3 or 4-4-2**. Secondary emergency shapes: **3-4-3 or 3-5-2**.

Reserve rule: slots 5–8 for defenders/midfielders and slots 4–6 for attackers prioritize expected minutes and a basic average-vote target of **MV ≥6.00**. Current `PV/MV` is an early-season signal, not a complete projection. Gabriel’s explicit preferences are preserved as marked exceptions.

Automated durability addition rule: a player omitted from the 176-player core enters the board when he has **PV = 3 in 2026/27** and **PV ≥30 in 2025/26**. If current **MV ≥6.00**, he enters the appropriate normal tier; otherwise he enters an explicit extra watchlist and does not displace a core player.

Manual promotions/demotions are locked and must not be changed by automated reviews.
